Open the email from Long Course and click the confirm link. Until you do, nothing is sent and your address is not on the list.

The confirmation usually arrives within a few hours (it is sent by the publication's assistant, which checks for new signups twice a day). If it never shows up, look in spam, or [write to us](/contact/).
