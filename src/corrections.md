## Report an error

If you think an article contains a factual error, a misread study, a broken source link, or a claim that has been overtaken by newer evidence, please [contact us](/contact/) and choose "A correction." Include the article title and the sentence in question. Reports go to a person, not only to an automated system.

## How corrections are handled

- **Factual errors** are corrected in the article, and a dated note at the bottom of the piece explains what changed.
- **Clarifications** that do not change meaning are made quietly, with the "updated" date refreshed.
- **Broken or replaced sources** are fixed, and the source list is updated.
- **Retractions.** If a piece cannot be made accurate, it is removed and this page records that it was.

We aim to acknowledge reports within a week.
