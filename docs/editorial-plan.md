# Long Course: 90 day editorial plan (22 Sep to 21 Dec 2026)

## Cadence

One substantial article per week, published Tuesdays at 7:00 AM Central, plus the newsletter the same morning. That is 13 articles in 90 days. Six are already drafted, so the backlog covers the first six weeks with a two article buffer by week 4 if one new draft is produced per week. Yigit is in his final semester with an internship, so the plan assumes his review time is about 15 minutes a week for standard-gate pieces and 30 to 45 minutes for the two human-gate pieces.

Quality over volume: if a piece is not ready, the slot is skipped and the newsletter is not sent. No filler.

## Themes in rotation

Fuel gets roughly 40 percent of slots (core audience), Everyday 25, Headlines 20, Flexibility 15. Flexibility pieces are never scheduled the same week as a piece with any energy or performance framing, and their newsletter issues never cross promote Fuel content.

## Newsletter welcome sequence (three emails)

1. **Immediately after confirmation: "What Long Course is."** One paragraph on the premise, the four lanes with one link each, the standards page, and a plain line about credentials (master's candidate and dietetic intern, not an RD). Ask one reply question: "What nutrition question do you wish someone would answer properly?" Replies feed the backlog.
2. **Day 3: "How to read us."** Explains the evidence, interpretation, practice separation and the accuracy review dates. Links the Headlines lane. No pitch.
3. **Day 7: "Start here."** Two or three articles matched to the reader's stated interest if they replied, otherwise the two most read pieces. Reminder that unsubscribing is one click.

## Measurement

Track weekly: unique visitors, returning visitors (GoatCounter), pages per visit, newsletter confirmations and unsubscribes (Buttondown), search impressions and clicks (Google Search Console once verified), and inbound messages by type. Review monthly which lanes retain readers and adjust the rotation, not the standards.

## Experiments (one at a time, two weeks each)

- Newsletter subject line style: question vs statement.
- Homepage lead: newest article vs editor's pick.
- Article length: 900 word explainer vs 1,600 word piece in the same lane.
Keep changes only when the difference is large enough to matter at this audience size; do not chase noise.
