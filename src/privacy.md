## The short version

Long Course does not run ads, does not sell data, and does not track you across the web. The site collects the minimum needed to publish a newsletter and answer messages.

## Analytics

Page views are counted with GoatCounter, a privacy focused analytics service that does not use cookies and does not build visitor profiles. It records the page visited, the referrer, screen size, and country level location derived from a hashed address that is not stored. This is used to learn which topics readers find useful.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is stored by Buttondown, the newsletter service used to send the letter. Subscription is double opt-in: you confirm by email before anything is sent. Every letter includes a one click unsubscribe link, and you can also [unsubscribe here](/newsletter/unsubscribe/). Your address is used only to send this newsletter.

## Contact form and email

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to the publication's inbox (hosted by Google) and are used to reply to you. An automated assistant sends a confirmation and may answer routine questions by pointing to published articles. Messages that need a human are read by Yiğit Aslan. Please do not send personal medical information; it is not needed to answer questions about the publication and we do not want to store it.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and operations for a limited time.

## Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be deleted, or unsubscribe at any time by [contacting us](/contact/).

## Educational disclaimer

Everything on Long Course is general education. It is not medical or nutritional advice, is not tailored to you, and does not replace guidance from a physician, registered dietitian, or other qualified professional who knows your situation. The author is a master's candidate and dietetic intern working toward the registered dietitian credential. If you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating, discuss any change in eating with your own care team.
